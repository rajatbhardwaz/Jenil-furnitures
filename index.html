Our Ethos

01 / Introduction

"Every space is a portrait of the people who inhabit it. We build those portraits — in wood, stone, and light."

Jenil Furnitures is not a showroom. We are a manufacturing studio that comes to your site, understands your architecture, and builds your interior from the ground up. From the first sketch to the final finish coat, every element is conceived, crafted, and installed by our team of artisan manufacturers and design specialists.

We work with natural walnut, white oak, teak veneer, engineered composites, brushed aluminium, ribbed glass, and architectural stone — selected not for trend, but for permanence. Our interiors are built to outlive the homes they inhabit.

02 / Materials

Solid Wood

Natural
Walnut

Sourced from FSC-certified European forests. Each slab is book-matched for grain continuity and dried for 18 months before fabrication to eliminate warping.

Veneer & Solid

White Oak
Veneer

Precision-cut 0.6mm veneers applied to engineered substrates. The grain direction is matched across every panel for a seamless, architectural surface.

Natural Stone

Beige
Travertine

Quarried from the Tivoli region of Italy. Used for countertops, backsplashes, and feature walls — honed to a matte finish that develops character over time.

Metal

Brushed
Bronze

Hand-brushed bronze profiles used for edge banding, handle rails, and architectural accents. Ages gracefully to deepen the warmth of surrounding timber.

Architectural Glass

Ribbed
Fluted Glass

Custom 10mm reeded glass panels used in cabinet doors, partition walls, and shelving units. Diffuses light while maintaining visual privacy and depth.

Metal Profile

Matte Black
Aluminium

Powder-coated 6061-T6 aluminium profiles used for framing, dividers, and structural elements. Lightweight, rigid, and architecturally precise.

Our Process

Made Where
You Live.

Every Jenil project is manufactured on-site by our team of specialist craftspeople. We bring our workshop to your space — measuring, cutting, veneering, jointing, and finishing within your home or commercial environment for a perfect architectural fit.

01

Material Curation & Sourcing

We visit timber yards, stone quarries, and metal fabricators with your project brief. Every slab, sheet, and profile is hand-selected for grain, density, and tonal harmony with your space.

02

On-site Framework Construction

Our master carpenters build the structural skeleton directly in your space — accounting for wall imperfections, floor levels, and architectural nuance that a factory-made unit could never accommodate.

03

Precision Veneering & Jointing

Veneers are applied in controlled conditions, grain-matched and edge-banded with precision. Each joint is invisible. Each corner is a 45° mitre. No shortcuts. No compromise.

04

Hand-finished Surface Coating

Final surfaces are hand-sanded through 12 grit stages and finished with either PU lacquer, natural oil wax, or water-based matte coatings — depending on the material and the desired feel.

Design Philosophy

The Detail
Is the Design.

We believe architecture exists at the scale of the hand. The quality of a space is felt in the moment you open a drawer, run your fingers across a panel, or observe how light falls across a grain. We obsess over every millimetre so you never have to.

⌀

Silent Soft-Close Mechanisms

Every door and drawer uses German Blum or Hettich hardware. No slamming, no bounce — just a quiet, controlled close that signals quality every single time.

≋

Book-matched Grain Continuity

Where veneers meet, we mirror the grain — so a cabinet looks like a single slice of tree, not an assembly of panels. This requires hand-sorting of every sheet before installation.

◯

Integrated Architectural Lighting

LED cove systems, recessed niche lighting, and under-cabinet strips are integrated into the structure itself — no exposed cables, no afterthought fixtures.

◫

Precision Joinery & Edge Profiles

Corners are hand-mitered to 45°. Edges are solid wood-banded and sanded flush. The boundary between two surfaces is not a gap — it's a line of intention.
